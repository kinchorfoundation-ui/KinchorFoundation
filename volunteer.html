Volunteer with us

Youth volunteers are paired one-on-one with a participant, giving dedicated support that makes every Fun Day safe, welcoming, and genuinely fun.

1

Reach out

Send us a message using the contact page — tell us a bit about yourself and when you're free.

Tap for more
We reply by email and can usually match you to the next available Fun Day within a week or two.
2

Get matched

We pair you one-on-one with a participant ahead of the event.

Tap for more
No experience with adapted sport is required — we'll give you a quick briefing on the day so you know how to support your buddy.
3

Show up and play

Meet your buddy, join the games, and help make the day a good one.

Tap for more
Groups stay small and sensory-friendly, and all equipment is provided — you just need to show up ready to have fun.

Why volunteers matter

Small groups and one-on-one pairing are what make our Fun Days work — every volunteer is what turns a sports day into a genuinely inclusive one. If you've got a free afternoon and want to make a real difference for a kid in your community, we'd love to hear from you.
