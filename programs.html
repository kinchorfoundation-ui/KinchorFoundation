Our Fun Days

We've run four Fun Days so far — soccer, basketball, and badminton — and every one is free, sensory-friendly, and pairs each participant with a dedicated volunteer. Tap a program to see the details.

⚽ Inclusive Soccer Fun Day

Simple, sensory-friendly games designed for players of every ability — red & green light, shooting on nets, British Bulldog, and a friendly scrimmage to finish. Small groups keep the environment calm and personal, and every player is paired one-on-one with a volunteer for the whole session.

Free Equipment provided 1:1 volunteer support
🏸 Inclusive Badminton Fun Day

A paired, youth-supported badminton session for players of every ability, with professional coaching from an experienced badminton coach. No experience needed — just come ready to rally and have fun.

Free Equipment provided Pro coaching
