🏸 Badminton Fun Day

August 17, 2026 · Bowness Community Gym

The gym doors open onto polished hardwood, and for a few hours the space belongs to badminton: nets strung low for the youngest players, rallies traded back and forth while the next group waits along the sideline.

Older kids and teen coaches run the sessions themselves, walking the younger ones through grips and footwork before letting them loose with a racquet — and, more often than not, a balloon standing in for a shuttlecock.

  • 5 participant players
  • 4 youth volunteers
  • Professional coaching · free · equipment provided

Every player left with a certificate. Most left with a balloon, too.

⚽ Soccer Fun Day

August 27, 2026 · Edgemont Park

Cones mark out the drills before anyone touches a ball: dribbling through a line of gates, then a pass, then a shot on the pop-up goals set up at either end of the grass.

Coaches — some barely older than the kids they're teaching — rotate through stations, calling out corrections and regrouping between rounds while a scrimmage runs in the background.

  • 9 participant players
  • 7 youth volunteers
  • Drills, scrimmage · free · equipment provided

By the end of the session, every ball on the field had found the back of a net at least once.

⚽ Soccer Fun Day

September 13, 2026 · Edgemont Park

By mid-September the jackets are out and the grass is damp, but it hasn't slowed anything down. Partner stretches open the session before the balls come out and the cones go down.

Pinnies split the group into teams, parents and older siblings watch from the sideline, and the drills give way to a full scrimmage by the end — chasing down loose balls in front of a pop-up goal until the light starts to go.

  • 7 participant players
  • 5 youth volunteers
  • Drills, scrimmage · free · equipment provided

Same field, new week — see you at the next one.

⚽🏀 Sports Fun Day

September 27, 2026 · Edgemont Park & school courts

One field, one afternoon, and enough energy for two sports. The grass hosts skills drills and small-sided soccer around a pair of pop-up goals, cones scattered across late-September grass already going gold at the edges.

When the group moves to the asphalt courts behind the school, the ball changes shape but the coaching doesn't skip a beat — the same mix of teen coaches and younger players, just cleats traded for sneakers.

  • Soccer + basketball, one afternoon
  • Teen coaches, all levels welcome
  • Free · equipment provided

Two sports, one very tired group by the end of it.

Four sessions, one growing program.
