About Kinchor Foundation

Empowering children and young people of all abilities, one Fun Day at a time.

Our mission

Kinchor Foundation exists to make sport accessible to every child and youth in Calgary, regardless of ability. We create small, sensory-friendly spaces where kids can play, connect, and be part of a team — free of charge, with a dedicated volunteer by their side.

We believe every kid should get the chance to run around, try a new sport, and be cheered on — not despite their needs, but with them fully in mind from the start.

Our Team

The people building Kinchor Foundation, Fun Day by Fun Day.

Hanry Zhang

Founder

I started Kinchor Foundation after seeing how often children and youth with special needs were left out or treated unequally in schools, especially during PE class. I wanted to build something different: a fun, welcoming space where kids of every ability could belong, be active, and simply enjoy being part of a team. That belief still drives every Fun Day we run.

Henry Jiang

Director of Operations

I helped start Kinchor Foundation because of my own experience growing up with special needs, including bullying and exclusion at school and in PE class. I know how it feels to be left out, and I didn't want other kids to go through the same thing. As Director of Operations, I help build Fun Days where every child feels included, supported, and valued.

Advisers

Jing Hao

Founder Adviser

As an Education Assistant, I work closely with children of diverse needs and understand how much inclusive opportunities matter for their sense of belonging. As Founding Adviser, I'm proud to support Hanry and Kinchor Foundation with my experience and perspective as the organization grows, helping build a community where every child can be active, gain confidence, and feel they belong.

Shining Shang

Program Coordinator Adviser

I hold a PhD in Mathematics and have over 25 years of experience in education, along with my perspective as a disability advocate, a mother of a child with autism, and an officer with the Navy League of Canada. As Program Coordinator Adviser, I bring that experience in education, advocacy, and community engagement to support Kinchor Foundation's mission of inclusive participation.
